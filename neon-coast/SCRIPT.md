# NEON COAST

*A short film. Running time about 6 minutes.*
*Shot for 4K DCI / UHD, 2.39:1 anamorphic, 23.976 fps. High-speed inserts at 120 fps.*

---

**LOOK:** Miami at dusk turning into night. Wet asphalt, Art Deco neon, palm silhouettes.
Teal and magenta for Mari, red and gold for Dax. Anamorphic flares, heavy film grain,
and deep blacks that are never crushed.

**SOUND:** A synth score that stays out of the way of the engines. The V4 howl, the
crackle when the throttle closes, and the tires singing on the bridge joints *are* the music.

---

### 1. EXT. LITTLE HAVANA — REYES MOTO SHOP — DUSK

*A rolling shutter is half down. Hand-painted sign above it: REYES MOTO — DESDE 1988.
Under the sign hangs a fresh notice: FORECLOSURE — PROPERTY OF HOLLOWAY CAPITAL.*

> **CAMERA:** 75mm anamorphic, locked off. The sign fills the frame. A drop of rain runs down the notice.

Inside, under one swinging bulb, the STRIX V4R sits on a paddock stand. It is matte
black and still ticking as it cools.

**MARISOL "MARI" REYES** (27, lean, a nose broken twice, an oil-stained undercut ponytail)
tightens the last axle nut. She wipes her hands on a rag and looks at an old photo taped to the
toolbox: her FATHER, younger, holding a trophy beside the same shop.

Her phone buzzes. A text from an unknown number:

> **TEXT:** *Ocean Drive. 9pm. Winner keeps the shop. — D.H.*

Mari reads it. She doesn't answer. She taps the tank twice.

> **INSERT (120 fps):** Two fingertips land on the tank. The teal STRIX lettering catches the bulb.

---

### 2. EXT. MIAMI STREETS — NIGHT — MONTAGE

*The bike leaves the shop. We never see the start, only the gate flinging open.*

> **CAMERA:** Russian Arm on a camera car, 24mm, low and close to the rear tire.
> Calle Ocho murals stream past in long-exposure smears.

- A domino player at Máximo Gómez Park looks up as she passes. The tiles rattle.
- The bike crosses the MacArthur Causeway. The cruise ships are lit like cities.
- **CLOSE:** Her visor. The skyline reflects across it, upside down.

---

### 3. EXT. OCEAN DRIVE, MIAMI BEACH — DUSK *(KEY FRAME: `art/scene_ocean_drive.png`)*

*Neon floods the Art Deco hotels: HOTEL, CAFE, OCEAN. Palms throw long shadows across
road still wet from an afternoon storm. A crowd lines the sidewalk with phones held up.*

At the far end, a red Italian-style V4 idles at the curb: **CORSA ROSSA**, plate DAX 01.

**DAX HOLLOWAY** (34, sun-bleached, a gold chain under red-chevron leathers) leans on it.
He smiles like he already owns the place, and most of it he does.

**DAX**
> Reyes. Your father rode this street like it owed him money.

**MARI**
> It did.

**DAX**
> Ocean Drive to the Causeway, Causeway to Key Biscayne. First to the lighthouse.
> You win, the shop's yours. I win —

*(He runs a finger along the Strix's fairing.)*

**DAX (CONT'D)**
> — you ride for me.

Mari pulls on her helmet. The iridium visor turns the whole street into a smear of neon.

**MARI** *(muffled)*
> Count it.

A STARTER GIRL with a Cuban flag steps between the two bikes. The crowd goes quiet.
The only sound is two idling V4s and the surf beyond the dunes.

> **CAMERA:** 135mm, compressed down the street. The sun sits on the vanishing point.
> The flag rises, holds, and falls.

---

### 4. THE LAUNCH

> **INSERT (120 fps):** The rear tire breaks loose. Smoke curls up through the magenta tail light.

> **INSERT:** Dax's gold visor. A smirk.

> **WIDE, CAMERA CAR, 2.39:1 (THE KEY FRAME):** We chase Mari from behind, low and centered.
> Dax's red tail light is already 50 meters ahead. Traffic light trails rip along both curbs.
> Palms flick past like a strobe.

Dax shifts with lazy precision. Mari stays in his draft, so close her front tire sits
inside his heat shimmer.

> **SOUND:** The score drops out completely. Only engines, wind, and her breathing inside the helmet.

---

### 5. EXT. 5TH STREET / MACARTHUR CAUSEWAY — NIGHT

They slingshot off Ocean Drive onto 5th Street. A police cruiser's lightbar lights up,
then gives up. The causeway opens out over black water.

> **CAMERA:** Drone, FPV, 14mm. It dives from the bridge lights down to six feet off the deck
> and chases them between the lanes.

Dax drifts into her line on purpose. Their elbows are inches apart.

> **INSERT:** His gloved hand flicks the hazard switch. His tail light strobes red straight into her eyes.

Mari flinches. She brakes, loses five lengths, and the gap opens.

---

### 6. EXT. RICKENBACKER CAUSEWAY — NIGHT

*The bridge to Key Biscayne climbs high and long. The city glitters behind it.*

Mari tucks in, chin on the tank and knees in. She taps the tank twice.

> **CLOSE (120 fps):** The tachometer needle sweeps into the red. The shift light flashes magenta.

She doesn't follow Dax's line. At the crest she takes the **outside**, the line her father
drew in the photo on the toolbox. The bike goes light over the top of the bridge, and both
wheels lift an inch off the joint.

> **WIDE (THE MONEY SHOT):** Profile, 200mm from a parallel boat on the bay.
> Both bikes are silhouetted against the Miami skyline. The Strix is airborne for twelve frames.
> Teal against red.

She lands, and she is alongside him.

---

### 7. EXT. CRANDON BLVD → BILL BAGGS PARK — NIGHT

Two lanes under a canopy of sea grapes. Sodium lamps flicker overhead. Dax looks over,
and the smile is gone.

**DAX** *(yelling into the wind)*
> You'll bin it!

Mari doesn't look at him. She has one corner left: a sharp right at the park gate.

> **CAMERA:** Tracking from the inside of the corner, 35mm. We see her knee slider
> kiss the asphalt and throw a spray of sparks across the lens.

Dax brakes late, too late. The Corsa Rossa runs wide into the sand shoulder and stalls.

Mari holds the apex.

---

### 8. EXT. CAPE FLORIDA LIGHTHOUSE — NIGHT

*The white tower rises over the dark beach. The beam sweeps once.*

The Strix rolls to a stop at its base. The engine ticks as it cools, the same sound as
the opening scene.

Mari takes off her helmet. She is breathing hard, sweat in her eyes. She laughs once,
almost a sob.

Headlights. Dax limps the red bike in and kills the engine. For a long moment he just
looks at her.

**DAX**
> ...Same line as your old man.

**MARI**
> No. He braked.

Dax pulls a folded deed from his jacket and holds it out. When she reaches for it,
he doesn't let go right away.

**DAX**
> Next time the stakes are higher.

**MARI**
> Next time bring a faster bike.

He lets go.

---

### 9. EXT. REYES MOTO SHOP — DAWN

*First light, pink and gold over Little Havana. The foreclosure notice is gone. The shutter
rolls all the way up.*

Mari hangs her helmet on a hook beside her father's old one. Then she taps the tank twice.

> **FINAL SHOT:** 24mm, slow push-in to the sign. The neon flickers on: REYES MOTO.
> The S buzzes, catches, and stays lit.

**SMASH TO BLACK.**

> **SOUND:** One V4 blip, then the crackle of the throttle closing.

**TITLE:** NEON COAST

## Production notes

- **Locations:** Ocean Drive (5th–15th St), MacArthur Causeway, Rickenbacker Causeway,
  Crandon Blvd, Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park, Calle Ocho. All riding is on closed roads
  with permits from the City of Miami Beach and the State Parks. Professional stunt riders only.
- **Colour pipeline:** ACES, with a custom "Neon Coast" LUT. Lift the shadows toward violet
  `#2a0b4f`, push the highlights toward peach `#ffb070`, and protect the teal `#19e6d0` and
  magenta `#ff2e88` as memory colours.
- **Delivery:** 4096×1716 DCI scope, and 3840×2160 UHD with letterbox (as in the key frame).
